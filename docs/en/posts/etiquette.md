# Vibe and etiquette

The ride rules are about nobody getting hurt. This post is about something else: making riding together a pleasure, and making a ride look like a ride and not a random crowd on bikes.

Slipping up on something here won't get you sent home, but it's what we should all be aiming for.

The mandatory stuff is in the [ride rules](/en/posts/rules), and how to ride in a group is in the [group riding guide](/en/posts/group-riding).

## How we look

We want every rider to be easy on the eyes, and the ride photos to be something we're not embarrassed to post. Road cycling is about aesthetics too, and we're not shy about it.

### Kit

- Clean, neat and the right size. Nothing flapping, billowing like a sail or see-through.
- Helmet without visors or mirrors.
- Socks of a sensible length. Not no-show socks that vanish into your shoes, and not knee-highs either - somewhere in between.
- Ideally, shaved legs. Not mandatory, but we'll notice.

### Bike

Clean, with a clean, lubed chain and neat bar tape. Bags, fenders and other add-ons - when the weather or the ride calls for them, not because you can't be bothered to take them off.

Mirrors on a bike are never a thing. Ever. If you're a worrier and want to know what's going on behind you, get a radar.

### Where all this comes from

A lot of the rules on aesthetics are collected at [velominati.com](https://www.velominati.com/the-rules/). Some of the rules there are straight-up unhinged, so we settle the debatable ones together in the chat. But as a starting point, it works.

### If you're new

You don't have to buy everything at once to come to your first ride. If you're not quite there yet on something or don't have something - no big deal, as long as you stay within the [mandatory rules](/en/posts/rules). The chat will always tell you what to get and where.

What matters is actually working towards the ideal, ride after ride. But the "ugh, I'm not a snob, I ride however I want" attitude doesn't really fly with us.

## How we communicate

### With each other

We help newcomers. We might have a little giggle, tease you a bit and drop a hint or two, but nothing harsh and no bullying. If you see someone doing something wrong in the group, tell them calmly, on the move or at a stop. Nobody needs a debrief with yelling.

### With drivers and pedestrians

Wave to people who let you through or passed you carefully.

For those who behave like pigs, feel free to throw a hand gesture, tell them what you think and explain what they did wrong: all that stress has to go somewhere. But no chasing and no fists - in a bike-vs-car argument we lose anyway.

### Litter

Don't toss wrappers, empty bottles or other trash wherever. Carry it in your pocket to the nearest bin or all the way home.

### Photos

We post ride photos and videos in the chat and on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/whatevercc.rs/), and everyone's going to be in them. If you don't want to be tagged, just say so and we won't tag you. But if you're genuinely scared of ending up in a shot, riding with us probably won't work out, sorry.

By default, by showing up for a ride you agree to photos and videos of you and to their use for any creative non-commercial purposes.

Nudes and compromising photos, if any happen to turn up, never get posted or shared anywhere. The only exception is the separate sauna chat.

## Chat

The chat features critical thinking (ha-ha) and jokes that go way over the line. We don't joke to be mean, we do it to let off steam: life's stressful enough as it is, and laughing at it is the best way not to go cuckoo. So don't take it personally, they're just jokes.

Questions about routes, bikes and kit can and should go there too: there are no stupid questions, only unfunny answers.
